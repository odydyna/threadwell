# Example plan: Wobbly Goat Analytics

A fictional customer, continuing the discovery example in the discovery call prep skill. Every name, number and date is made up.

```
ONBOARDING PLAN · Wobbly Goat Analytics · Signed 2026-11-02
Sponsor: Priya Okafor, Head of People   Admin: Tom Reyes, People Ops   Seats: 240

HANDOFF SUMMARY
Bought: annual plan, 240 seats, offsite planning included.
Why now: three senior engineers quit last quarter; virtual escape rooms fell
from 80% to 15% attendance.
Promised: first experience within 30 days; engineering included from the start.
Risks: engineers see "culture stuff" as marketing's thing; two time zones
(US and Portugal); the team is tired of initiatives.

SUCCESS, IN THEIR WORDS (draft, confirm at kickoff)
1. No more senior engineering resignations citing disconnection this year.
2. At least 60% of teams join an experience each month by month 3.
3. Engineers rate "I can get help from other teams" higher on the next survey.

PLAN FOR SKEPTICS
Engineers · Might resist: "another forced fun thing" · Frame it as: fewer
handoff headaches with product and sales · Wins them over: a first session
built around a real cross team problem, not games.
Managers · Might resist: no time · Frame it as: help with retention, which is
already their problem · Wins them over: a 30 minute preview, not a training.
Leadership · Might resist: unclear return · Frame it as: the cost of replacing
three senior engineers · Wins them over: a simple monthly retention and
attendance view.
Everyone · Might resist: initiative fatigue · Frame it as: optional, short,
useful · Wins them over: the first session ending on time with one practical
takeaway.

30 DAY PLAN
Week 1: admin setup with Tom; kickoff call; success measures agreed.
Week 2: manager preview session; first experience chosen with Priya.
Week 3: invitations out (opt in); facilitator confirmed; time slots for both
time zones.
Week 4: first experience delivered; pulse survey sent.
Next 60 days: experience 2 (engineering and product together); experience 3
(manager session on leading across generations); 90 day check in with Priya.

FACILITATOR MATCHES
Experience 1 · Facilitator with an engineering background who runs practical
problem solving sessions · Engineers trust someone who speaks their language.
Experience 2 · Facilitator skilled in cross team collaboration · The real
friction sits between engineering and product.
Experience 3 · Leadership coach experienced with mixed age teams · Managers
asked for help relating to younger hires.

KICKOFF AGENDA (45 min)
0:00  Introductions and why Threadwell (CSM)
0:05  What's happened so far, in Priya's words (Priya)
0:15  Agree success measures (CSM and Priya)
0:25  Skeptics and how we'll bring them in (CSM)
0:35  30 day plan and owners (CSM and Tom)
0:42  Next steps (CSM)

WELCOME EMAIL (draft)
Subject: Welcome to Threadwell. Here's your first 30 days

Hi Priya and Tom,

Welcome to Threadwell. We're glad you're here.

Our goal together: your first team experience within 30 days. Here's what
happens next:
- Kickoff call on [date] (45 minutes). We'll agree what success looks like
  for Wobbly Goat.
- Tom, I'll send a short setup checklist after the call.
- Week 2, a 30 minute preview for your managers.

Before the kickoff, could you share your latest engagement survey highlights,
if you have them?

Talk soon,
[CSM name]

GAPS
Latest engagement survey; CEO's view of the resignations; best meeting times
for the Portugal team.
```
