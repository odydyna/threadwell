# Example brief: Wobbly Goat Analytics renewal

A fictional customer, one year after the onboarding example in the onboarding kickoff skill. Every name, number and date is made up.

```
RENEWAL BRIEF · Wobbly Goat Analytics · Renewal 2027-11-02
Sponsor: Priya Okafor, Head of People   Seats: 240   Risk: medium

SUCCESS MEASURES
1. "No more senior engineering resignations citing disconnection."
   Start: 3 in the quarter before signing · Now: 1 senior engineer left,
   citing relocation · Met
2. "At least 60% of teams join an experience each month by month 3."
   Start: 15% (escape rooms) · Month 3: 58% · Month 8: 71% · Partly met,
   then exceeded
3. "Engineers rate 'I can get help from other teams' higher."
   Start: 3.1 out of 5 · Now: 3.8 out of 5 · Met

VALUE STORY
Since Threadwell started, engineering and product have met monthly in
facilitated sessions built around real problems between their teams.
Alongside that, engineering resignations tied to disconnection stopped and
the cross team help score rose 0.7 points.
What didn't work: Portugal attendance stayed near 40%. Sessions were timed
for US afternoons. In month 6 we added a Lisbon friendly slot, and Portugal
attendance has climbed since.

RENEWAL RISK
Signals: Priya starts parental leave in month 10, with Tom covering. Company
grew from 240 to 275 people, so 35 people aren't covered yet. No complaints
open. Overall: medium, because the sponsor will be away during the renewal
window.

RECOMMENDATION
Renew and expand to 275 seats, with Lisbon time experiences built into the
calendar. Why: participation is strong, all three measures are met, and the
newest hires are the ones most at risk of feeling disconnected.

LIKELY OBJECTIONS
"Budget is tight this year." · Answer: compare the subscription with the
cost of replacing even one senior engineer. Offer to walk finance through it.
"Can't we run this ourselves now?" · Answer: possibly. Show what happened
before (15% escape room attendance) and what the platform does monthly:
planning, facilitator matching and measurement.
"Portugal uses it less." · Answer: true, and here's what we changed in month
6 and how attendance has moved since.

TIMELINE
2027-07-05 (120 days): prep starts, data pulled
2027-08-04 (90 days): value review with Priya and Tom, before Priya's leave
2027-09-03 (60 days): renewal proposal to Tom, Priya copied
2027-10-03 (30 days): signature target

VALUE REVIEW OUTLINE
1. Where you started: the three resignations and the 15% attendance
2. What we agreed success would look like
3. Results against each measure
4. What didn't work, and what we changed
5. What your team said (team level themes)
6. The next year: newer hires, Lisbon time slots, the annual offsite

INVITATION EMAIL (draft)
Subject: A year of Threadwell at Wobbly Goat. Time to look back together

Hi Priya and Tom,

It's almost a year since we started together, and I'd love to walk you
through what's changed: the three goals we set at kickoff, where we hit them,
and where we adjusted.

Could we find 45 minutes in early August, before Priya's leave? I'll bring
the numbers. You bring the questions.

Talk soon,
[CSM name]

GAPS
Latest company wide engagement survey; finance contact for the renewal;
whether the Lisbon slot trend holds through the summer.
```
