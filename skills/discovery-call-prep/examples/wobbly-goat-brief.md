# Example brief: Wobbly Goat Analytics

A fictional prospect, invented to show this skill's tone, length and structure. Every fact, name and source below is made up. In a real brief, each fact carries a real source.

```
DISCOVERY BRIEF · Wobbly Goat Analytics · 2026-10-30
Contact: Priya Okafor, Head of People

SNAPSHOT
- Data analytics software for independent grocery stores. Mascot: a goat
  named Gerald. (company website)
- 240 employees, up from 150 a year ago. (sales notes)
- Fully remote across the US and Portugal. No office since 2022. (careers page)
- Three senior engineers resigned in the last quarter. (sales notes)
- Tried monthly virtual escape rooms. Attendance fell from 80% to 15% in four
  months. (Priya, inbound form)
- Values on the website: "Curious, kind, a little weird." (company website)

WHY NOW: THREE HYPOTHESES
1. Fast hiring has diluted the early culture. Confidence: high
   Evidence: 60% growth in a year; the original team now sits in a sea of
   new faces.
2. Engineers and everyone else have drifted into two separate worlds.
   Confidence: medium
   Evidence: the people who left were all engineers; the escape rooms were
   run by the marketing team.
3. Their connection efforts feel like forced fun. Confidence: medium
   Evidence: escape room attendance collapsed after the novelty wore off.

QUESTIONS TO ASK
Fast hiring
- What did the culture feel like at 50 people that it doesn't now?
- How do new hires find out how things really work here?
Two worlds
- When did engineering last work closely with another team? How did it go?
- What did the engineers who left say in their exit conversations?
Forced fun
- Which connection efforts did people actually enjoy, and why?
- What made the escape rooms stop working?
Sponsor
- Who besides you would need to back this?
Success
- A year from now, what would tell you this worked?

FIT CHECK
Green: in the size range; fully remote; a Head of People who reached out
herself; clear trigger.
Yellow: two countries means two time zones; "a little weird" culture may
resist anything that feels corporate.
Red: none found.

LISTEN FOR
- Whether Priya says "we" or "they" when she talks about engineering.
- Fatigue with yet another initiative.
- Whether Gerald the goat is a real culture asset or a forced mascot.

GAPS
Engagement survey results, budget owner, how the CEO sees the resignations.
```
