# Renewal prep skill · timed test

Goal: a real, measured result for the portfolio, resume and interviews. Takes about 20 minutes.

You need: the Claude app with the renewal prep skill on, the file `wobbly-goat-renewal-data.md` (in this folder), and a timer (your phone is fine).

## Step 1 · Your baseline (2 minutes, before you start)

Think back to preparing a renewal or QBR at Vibes or On the Map: pulling the numbers, checking each goal, writing the story, risks, timeline, and the invitation email.

**How long did one renewal prep usually take you by hand?** ______ hours

Be honest and a little conservative. This is the number you'll compare against.
